Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns across the planet. Human activities, mainly burning fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas, have been the main driver of climate change since the 1800s. Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases that trap heat in the atmosphere and raise global temperatures. Rising sea levels, melting glaciers and more frequent heatwaves are some of the visible effects of a warming world. Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power can help reduce carbon emissions.
