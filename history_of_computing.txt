The first general purpose electronic computer, ENIAC, was completed in 1945 and filled an entire room. The invention of the transistor in 1947 made computers smaller, faster and more reliable. Personal computers became popular in the 1980s and brought computing into homes and offices. The World Wide Web, created by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989, transformed how people share information. Smartphones today carry more computing power than the machines that guided astronauts to the moon.
